# Pre-Jev report and credit recommendation

Date: 2026-10-05. Plan 1, task 5.

## What the research found

- 46 candidate categories researched with sources (38 seeded, 8 added for Asia Pacific coverage).
  A fact-check opened 256 load-bearing citations; 232 (91%) held. Corrections are in
  `research/fact_checks.csv`.
- A skeptical re-grade cut the legacy test from "every category passes" to a real filter.
  Rule: at least one strong sign and strong + half weak >= 2.5. **35 categories kept, 11 dropped**
  (`research/categories.csv`). Only 3 categories have 3 strong signs: custom COBOL and mainframe,
  government benefits, government licensing and permits.
- 289 challengers and 378 vendor products mapped (`research/challengers.csv`, `research/vendors.csv`).
- 123 free sources mapped across 11 regions (`research/sources.csv`).

## What was counted

| Family | Sources with totals | Items (upper bound) |
|---|---|---|
| Tenders | USAspending, TED, Contracts Finder, GeBIZ | 10,035 |
| Job APIs | Bundesagentur, MyCareersFuture | 3,693 |

| SI stories | Capgemini, Cognizant, Deloitte, Wipro | 3,846 |
| HN | local 4M-comment snapshot | 3,197 |

Counts are per query, so an item matching several queries is counted more than once.

Problems found and handled:
- Four APIs first returned whole databases for multi-word queries. Fixed with exact-phrase syntax and a
  sanity guard. EURES and Kalibrr still match loosely, so all their counts are rejected.
- TED "Maximo" (58,440) matched the Romance-language word for "maximum"; dropped.
- Not reachable for free and polite access: Naukri, Glints, Reddit (robots), SAP and Tyler story pages
  (robots), JobThai, Careers@Gov (no static total), Epic, Finastra, Infosys, TCS, ServiceNow (no usable
  sitemap or 403). India and the EU job market are the weakest regions in the counting lane.
- HN barely discusses these systems: 1,590 matching comments in 4M, 1,235 of them COBOL or mainframe.

## Jev estimate

From `uv run lsa estimate` with `configs/passes.toml` (full output in `2026-10-05-jev-estimate.json`):
**$2.11** for 7 passes over 141,000 items and about 50M input tokens at $0.042 per million.

Adjustments:
- The HN study's billed tokens ran up to 2x the byte estimate for some question families: x2 = $4.22.
- Pilot, blind-audit replay and reruns: about $2.

**Recommended credit: $10, with `account_total = 8.00`.** That is a third of the $25 target and leaves
room for the uncounted sources if access improves.
